How these numbers are computed
  • Active time adds up the gaps between a grader's events inside one submission. A gap longer than the idle cap counts as the cap. Time between Pause and Resume is skipped.
  • Copy-to-copy is the gap between two consecutive copies in a submission: reading the next part of the work, ticking deductions and pasting the previous comment. The console can't see the paste in Canvas, so this stands in for copy-to-paste time.
  • First copy is the time from opening a submission to the first comment copied, mostly reading time.
  • Unfinished submissions (no Next submission yet) are counted separately and left out of the averages.
